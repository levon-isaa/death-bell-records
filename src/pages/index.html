    About

    Yerevan, Armenia

    Death Bell Records is an independent techno label from Yerevan, founded by DJ, producer and sound engineer Tøtal.

    The catalogue runs through hard groove, industrial and hypnotic techno: tight grooves, heavy low end, dry drums. Alongside the releases, the label runs Death Bell Nights and the DBR Podcast, connecting Yerevan's underground with artists from around the world. In June 2026 it celebrated ten years with its residents at The How.
